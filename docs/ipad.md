# Using Car Scratch on an iPad

Car Scratch runs in Safari on an iPad. You do not install anything from the App Store. You only need
to be on the same home Wi-Fi as the car.

## Open it

1. Make sure the car is switched on and the owner has set it up.
2. In Safari, open **http://picar.local:8080**.
   - If that address does not load, ask the owner for the car's IP address and use
     `http://<that address>:8080` instead.
3. Turn the iPad to **landscape** (sideways). Car Scratch is made for landscape.

## Add it to the Home Screen (recommended)

1. Tap the **Share** button in Safari.
2. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Car Scratch from the new icon. It opens full screen, without the Safari address bar.

Opening from the Home Screen can be a little different from Safari's tabs. If the car shows as
disconnected after you switch apps, tap **Resume** when it asks.

## Layout

- **Scratch blocks** are on the left. Choose a category, then drag a block into the script area with
  your finger.
- The bottom-right pane has two tabs:
  - **Sprites**: the Scratch sprite list.
  - **Pi Car**: the car monitor. It shows the car's position, the sensor cone, the line sensors,
    the light sensors and any warning banners.
- The red **E-STOP** button is always on screen in the menu bar and over the stage.

## Drag blocks

- Touch and hold a block, then drag it into a script. Let go to drop it.
- To remove a block, drag it back to the block palette.
- Scratch's usual touch gestures work for blocks. There is no keyboard on the iPad, so use the
  on-screen **Touch remote** example to drive the car by tapping.

## Examples

- Open the **Examples** menu in the menu bar and choose one.
- **Touch remote** is the easiest first example. It shows buttons for forward, backward, left,
  right and stop, plus a speed slider and a distance reading.

## Save and open programs

- **File > Save to car…** stores the program on the car, so it is still there next time.
- **File > Open from car…** loads a program that was saved earlier.
- On an iPad, use these instead of the normal file save. Your program is kept on the car, not on the
  iPad.

## Switching apps, locking the screen, or leaving the tab

When you switch away from Safari or Car Scratch, lock the iPad, or leave the tab, the iPad pauses the
page. Car Scratch cannot keep the car running in the background, so:

- The car **stops within about one second** and **pauses**.
- When you come back, Car Scratch reconnects automatically. The car does **not** start moving again on
  its own.
- Tap **Resume** to start the program again. Check the car and the floor first.

## Sound

- Audio needs one tap before it can play. Tap anywhere on the screen once after opening Car Scratch.

## Screen size

- Car Scratch fits the screen on smaller iPads, such as the iPad mini or the older 9.7-inch model.
  The page is scaled to fit, so the buttons may look smaller.
- If the layout looks wrong, close the app and open it again in landscape.
- In portrait, Car Scratch shows a "rotate your iPad" hint. Turn the iPad to landscape to continue.

## Tips

- Keep your fingers off the moving car when the program runs.
- Use the red **E-STOP** button if anything looks wrong. Only the controller can press **Reset**
  afterwards.
- Only one device can control the car at a time. A second device can watch and press E-STOP, but
  cannot drive. Ask the owner if you need to take control.
- Read `docs/safety.md` before you run a program on the floor.
