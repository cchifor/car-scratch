# Car Scratch: a guide for young programmers

Car Scratch lets you program a real robot car with blocks. You snap blocks together, press the green
flag, and the car does what your program says.

## What the car can do

- Drive forward and backward at different speeds.
- Turn left, turn right, and drive straight.
- Measure how far away something is, using its "eyes" at the front.
- Notice a line on the floor and follow it.
- Notice a light and turn towards it.
- Stop by itself when something is in its way.

## The 5 most important blocks

1. **drive forward at 50 %**: starts the car moving forward.
2. **stop motors**: stops the car.
3. **steer left** and **steer right**: turns the front wheels.
4. **distance (cm)**: tells you how far away the thing in front is.
5. **when obstacle closer than 20 cm**: a hat block. Your script runs when something gets close.

## Safety rules

1. Always keep your fingers, hair and cables away from the wheels.
2. Lift the car off the floor and test a new program before it drives on the floor.
3. Ask a grown-up to be there when the car drives.
4. Press the big red **E-STOP** button if anything looks wrong.
5. If the car stops and waits, press **Resume** only after you have checked the floor.

## Three tiny challenges

1. **Square dance.** Make the car drive forward for 1 second, turn right, and repeat four times.
   Can you make it come back to where it started?
2. **Stop sign.** Make the car drive forward until the distance is less than 30 cm, then stop.
   Try it with two different starting points.
3. **Speed race.** Make the car drive forward for 2 seconds at 30 %, then 2 seconds at 60 %. Which
   distance is longer?

Good luck, and have fun!
